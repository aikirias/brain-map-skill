# Team Working Agreement — Engineering

How we treat each other:

- Core hours 10:00–16:00; async otherwise.
- Disagree-and-commit after a decision is logged.
- No silent PRs older than 2 days.
- Psychological safety: it's fine to say 'I don't know'.
- Friday demo, no slides required.
Owned by [[Petra Novak]]; reflects *Earn trust in small steps* and *Default to candor*.
